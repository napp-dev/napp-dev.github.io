1. Using Weekwall

Weekwall turns selected calendar events and a background into wallpaper for your iPhone. Use only images and content you are entitled to use. You retain your rights in your content. Calendar permission and Apple Shortcuts are needed for the corresponding features. Follow the in-app setup to install the supplied shortcut and configure automatic updates.

A wallpaper is a snapshot, not a live calendar or a guaranteed reminder. Check your calendar for the latest schedule. Automatic updates depend on iOS, permissions, the installed shortcut and your automation settings. Event information can be visible on the Lock Screen; use the privacy options when appropriate.
