Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Glimmer — Affirmations handles information. The developer responsible for the app is identified on its App Store page. Contact: napp.dev.support@gmail.com.

1. Information on your device

There is no developer account or sign-in. Your own affirmations, favourites, viewing history, selected topics and goals, grammatical gender, appearance and reminder preferences are stored on your device. The widget reads a local snapshot through shared app storage. We do not operate an account-based cloud service for this content. Apple device backups depend on your settings.

Reminders are local notifications and require your permission. Affirmation text may appear in notifications and widgets on your device; choose your notification preview settings accordingly. Sharing opens the iOS share sheet and sends the content you choose to the recipient or service you select. The app does not request access to your contacts, microphone or precise location.

2. Analytics, diagnostics and configuration

Google Analytics for Firebase collects app interactions and technical information. Our onboarding events include the step viewed, chosen grammatical gender, number of selected goals, notification permission outcome, completion, flow and duration. Paywall events include display, closing, purchase or restore outcome and selected plan. Our custom analytics events do not include the text of your own affirmations or the names of selected goals.

Technical data can include device model, app and operating-system versions, language, approximate region, session information and app-instance or installation identifiers. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, diagnostic information and associated installation identifiers. Firebase Remote Config uses installation identifiers to deliver app configuration, including the minimum supported app version. These services help us understand usage, fix failures and maintain the app. The current app has no separate switch to disable all analytics and crash reporting.

3. Advertising and purchases

The app uses Google AdMob. Its SDK may process IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device and app identifiers, diagnostics, ad impressions, interactions and advertising measurements to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is available only with iOS tracking permission. The current version does not request that permission or provide an in-app advertising consent form; do not assume that all SDK data collection depends on a tracking prompt. Details: AdMob data disclosures and Google Privacy Policy.

Apple processes Premium purchases. The app receives transaction information to verify access, including the product and subscription validity. Purchase-related events can also be processed by Analytics as described above. We do not receive your full payment-card details or Apple Account password. Premium does not disable analytics or crash reporting.

4. AI-assisted content

Some built-in content was created with the help of artificial intelligence before publication. The current app does not send your own affirmation text to an AI service to generate responses. Adding a personal affirmation saves it locally. Sharing it or sending it to support discloses the information you choose through those channels.

5. Purposes, legal grounds and recipients

We process information to provide app features and purchases, maintain reliability, understand usage, configure the app, fund free access through advertising and respond to support requests. Depending on the processing and applicable law, legal grounds may include performance of a contract, consent where required, legal obligations, or legitimate interests in maintaining and improving the app where permitted. Using the app or accepting these terms does not replace any separate consent required by law.

Recipients include Apple for purchases, Google for Firebase and AdMob, advertising partners involved in ad delivery, and the email provider used for support. Providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Applicable safeguards and processing terms depend on the service and jurisdiction. See Firebase privacy and security, Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and security

Local information remains until you delete it or remove the app; backup copies follow your Apple backup settings. User-level Analytics data follows the retention period configured for the Google Analytics property; aggregated reports may remain longer. Firebase documents a 90-day retention period for crash reports and associated identifiers before deletion begins. Remote Config installation identifiers remain until a deletion request is made to Firebase. Advertising data follows Google's retention rules linked above. Support messages are retained as needed to resolve requests and meet legal obligations.

Removing the app stops new app activity but does not immediately erase information previously received by service providers. Network services use encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission guarantees absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You can delete your own affirmations in the app, remove favourites, change preferences and manage notification permission and previews in iOS Settings. Removing the app deletes its local app storage, subject to device backups and shared widget storage managed by iOS. Where a tracking permission exists, manage it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. This setting does not switch off all analytics, diagnostics or advertising data processing.

Depending on your country, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive personal data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. Send requests to napp.dev.support@gmail.com. We may need information that links a request to an installation; a name or email address alone may not identify SDK data. We will explain available steps and respond within applicable legal time limits. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing.

8. Children, support and this website

The app is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal data can contact us.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and attachments. The app's support email draft includes app version, iOS version and device model; you can edit it before sending. Please avoid sending sensitive health information, passwords or payment credentials.

These documentation pages use no advertising SDKs, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, logs visitors' IP addresses for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External sites and your email provider have their own policies.

9. Changes and contact

We publish revisions at this address with an updated date and provide additional notice or request consent where required. Questions: napp.dev.support@gmail.com.

Some in-app content was created with the help of artificial intelligence. Affirmations are for general inspiration and do not replace professional medical or psychological care.
