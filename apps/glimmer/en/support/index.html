Need help with Glimmer — Affirmations? Email napp.dev.support@gmail.com. For a bug, include your device model, iOS and app versions, what happened and what you expected. You can edit the technical details included in the app's email draft before sending. Remove personal information from screenshots. Never send passwords or full payment-card details.

Restore Premium

Open Settings → Restore purchases, or use Restore on the Premium purchase screen, with the Apple Account used for purchase. Check your connection and subscription validity. If access is still missing, contact us with the product name and purchase date. Restoring Premium does not restore personal affirmations or favourites.

Cancel a subscription or request a refund

Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions and select Glimmer. You can also use the app's subscription management option for an active subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Cancel a trial at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid a charge. Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com. See Apple's cancellation instructions. The one-time Premium purchase has no recurring charge.

Personal affirmations and favourites

Your content is stored on your device, with no developer account or cloud synchronisation. Deleting or reinstalling the app can remove it; Apple device backups depend on your settings. We cannot retrieve a separate server copy. The current app does not send your own phrases to an AI service to generate content.

Reminders, widgets and language

Check notification permission for Glimmer, Focus settings and your chosen reminder schedule in the app. Widget updates are scheduled by iOS and may be delayed. Choose the app's interface language in iOS app settings. Documentation is available in all 11 app languages: English, Russian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Turkish, Japanese and Korean. English is the fallback for unsupported languages.

Content or privacy questions

Report an unsuitable phrase with its text or topic and app language. Some built-in content was created with AI assistance. Affirmations do not replace medical or psychological care. Support email is not an emergency service.

For privacy requests, write to napp.dev.support@gmail.com. Please avoid unnecessary sensitive health information. The Privacy Policy explains data handling; the Terms of Use describe subscriptions and use of the app.

Some in-app content was created with the help of artificial intelligence. Affirmations are for general inspiration and do not replace professional medical or psychological care.
